Research

Reliable AI for uncertain, incomplete, real-world data

My research develops trustworthy artificial intelligence methods for Alzheimer's disease and related dementia research, spanning generative modeling (for imputing and harmonizing medical imaging data across sites), formal verification of algorithmic fairness, and uncertainty quantification and safety guardrailing for large language models — with the goal of building medical AI systems that are provably reliable, fair, and safe for real clinical and research use.
